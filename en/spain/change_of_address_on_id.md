## Description
### What is it?
A change of address on ID is a mandatory legal requirement when you move to a new residential address in Spain. Under Real Decreto 1155/2024 (effective May 20, 2025), all foreign residents must notify authorities of address changes within one month. This process involves two key steps: updating your municipal registration (empadronamiento) and notifying the National Police of your new address. The NIE (Número de Identificación de Extranjero) itself is a permanent identification number that never changes, but your registered address in the system must be kept current for legal compliance, tax purposes, and official communications.

### Who needs it?
This procedure is mandatory for:
- All foreign residents in Spain holding a NIE (white A4 document)
- EU citizens with registration certificates (green certificate)
- Non-EU residents with TIE cards (Tarjeta de Identidad de Extranjero)
- Any foreigner with residence or stay authorization who changes their domicile
- Spanish citizens returning from abroad who need to update their address

Failure to update your address within 30 days can result in administrative sanctions and complications with renewals, tax filings, and accessing public services.

## Procedure

### Step 1: Municipal Registration (Empadronamiento)
1. **Schedule an appointment** at your local Town Hall (Ayuntamiento) - many municipalities require prior appointments
2. **Visit the Padrón office** at your city's Town Hall with all required documentation
3. **Complete the census form** (Hoja Padronal) with all sections properly filled
4. **Submit documentation** - If approved, you'll receive confirmation within 7 calendar days
5. **Request multiple copies** (3-4) of your census certificate (certificado de empadronamiento) as you'll need them for other procedures

### Step 2: Update NIE/TIE at Police Station (As of May 2025)
1. **Important change**: Under Real Decreto 1155/2024, address changes are NO LONGER processed at Immigration Offices (Oficina de Extranjería). You must go to the Police Station (Comisaría de Policía) in your new locality
2. **Book appointment (Cita Previa)**: 
   - Visit [sede.administracionespublicas.gob.es](https://sede.administracionespublicas.gob.es/pagina/index/directorio/icpplus)
   - Select your province and "Policía - Cambio de domicilio"
   - Appointments fill quickly - check frequently or refresh (F5) if none available
3. **Attend appointment** with all documentation
4. **Pay applicable fees** (Tasa 790 código 012) if required
5. **Receive confirmation** of address update in the system

### Online Alternative (With Digital Certificate)
If you have a digital certificate, Cl@ve PIN, or Cl@ve Permanente, you can notify multiple authorities simultaneously through:
- [Cambio de Domicilio platform](https://cambiodomicilio.redsara.es)
- This notifies: DGT (Traffic), Social Security, Tax Agency (Agencia Tributaria), and Police Registry

### Processing Timeline
- **Deadline**: You must notify changes within 30 days of moving
- **Empadronamiento**: 7 days for confirmation
- **Police notification**: Immediate update in system
- **New TIE card** (if applicable): 1-2 months

## Required Documents

### For Empadronamiento (Municipal Registration):
**Personal identification:**
- Valid passport or current NIE/TIE (original)
- Properly completed census form (Hoja Padronal) with all sections filled

**Proof of residence** (one of the following):
- **Property owners**: Property Registry Note or Property Deed (if purchased within last year), OR latest Property Tax receipt (IBI) for older properties
- **Renters**: Valid rental contract for residential use PLUS latest rent receipt in your name
- **Utility bills**: Recent electricity, water, gas, or landline phone bill in your name

**Additional requirements if not owner/primary renter:**
- Written authorization from an adult registered at the address who is the property owner
- Copy of the authorizing person's ID
- If owner not registered at address: responsible declaration from owner/tenant authorizing registration + proof of ownership

### For NIE/TIE Address Update at Police Station:
- **Current documentation**: Valid passport, NIE, or TIE card
- **Census certificate**: Updated empadronamiento certificate (issued less than 3 months ago)
- **Recent photograph**: Passport-size, color photo with white background
- **Payment receipt**: Tasa 790 código 012 payment confirmation (if applicable)
- **Forms**: Completed EX-17 form (for TIE modifications) or appropriate form for your case

### Fees (2025 rates):
- **Empadronamiento**: Free of charge
- **Address notification**: Free for simple address updates
- **TIE card renewal/modification**: €16.08-€21.87 depending on type (Tasa 790 código 012)
- **Payment methods**: Tasa must be paid in person at authorized banks - online payment not available

## Additional details

### Important Tips and Common Issues

**Document Organization:**
- Keep scanned digital copies of all documents in a dedicated folder
- Have multiple photocopies ready - Spanish offices often require several copies
- Double-check all entries (passport numbers, NIE) to avoid processing delays

**Common Mistakes to Avoid:**
- **Don't confuse documents**: NIE (white A4 paper), TIE (plastic card), and EU Registration Certificate (green paper) are different documents with different procedures
- **Rural address issues**: If living in rural areas (campo), you may face mail delivery problems as authorities use domicile addresses even when mail isn't delivered there
- **Timing**: Don't wait - you have only 30 days to notify changes to avoid sanctions

**Money-Saving Tips:**
- Address changes on NIE documents are free for simple notifications
- Consider updating during annual tax filing as an alternative to separate procedure
- When paying Tasa 790, choose higher amount if unsure - overpayment is accepted, underpayment requires resubmission

**After Address Change:**
Update your address with these essential services:
- **Banking**: All bank accounts and credit cards
- **Insurance**: Health, car, home, and life insurance policies  
- **Employment**: HR department for tax withholdings and social security
- **Healthcare**: GP registration, specialist appointments, prescriptions
- **Education**: Schools, universities, student aid programs
- **Driving license**: Notify DGT (free online with digital certificate)
- **Vehicle registration**: Update car/motorcycle registration
- **Utilities**: Transfer or set up new accounts (electricity, gas, water, internet)
- **Postal service**: Set up mail forwarding from old address
- **Subscriptions**: Update all delivery services, magazines, online shopping accounts

### Legal Consequences of Non-Compliance
- Administrative fines ranging from €501-€10,000 for failure to notify address changes
- Complications with residence permit renewals
- Issues with tax filings and potential penalties
- Problems accessing public services and benefits
- Difficulties with official correspondence and legal notifications

### Special Situations
- **Moving between provinces**: You must submit TIE renewals in the province where you first received authorization (as of 2025)
- **Temporary moves**: Address changes are still required even for temporary relocations over 30 days
- **Multiple residences**: Primary residence address should be registered based on where you spend most time
- **International moves**: Additional procedures required if moving outside Spain while maintaining Spanish residence status
